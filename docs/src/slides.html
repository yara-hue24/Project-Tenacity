Project Tenacity

Plan the walk on Mars before you take it

The problem

On Mars, the limit of a walk is not distance. It is getting back to shelter in time.

60 minis how long a suit's backup life support lasts if the main system fails. You must never be further from the refuge than that.
20°is the steepest slope a suit is required to handle: up, down or across. A map that looks flat can hide steeper ground.
No rescueand no satellite navigation. Oxygen, cooling water, battery, daylight and solar storms all have to be right before the airlock opens.

A crew needs one answer before they step outside: can we go, and get home?

Sources: NASA xEMU life-support description (NTRS 20170009505); NASA Artemis III EVA mission capability briefing (NTRS 20210026296).

The idea

Rover drivers simulate every drive. Astronauts should too.

At JPL, every rover drive is sketched, simulated and checked before it is sent to Mars. Tenacity gives a crew on foot the same loop.

How it works

Click the map. The terrain engine finds the way and shows why.

1Sketch. Click anywhere to add a station.
2Path. A 5-million-cell elevation grid, in the browser, finds the easiest walk. Red cannot be crossed, yellow is slow.
3Simulate. Pace on every slope, oxygen, water, battery, and the walk-home time from every point.
4Validate. Rules name what blocks the walk and where. Missing data is "not evaluated", never a pass.

A sketched station: the dashed white line is the straight line the path finder rejected; the route goes around ground steeper than 20°.

See it before you walk it

A 3D flyover of the real ground, with Curiosity's own photo of where you are going.

  • Real terrain. HiRISE elevation draped with HiRISE imagery; heights exaggerated 1.5 times and labelled so.
  • Real views. The photo Curiosity took at each station appears as the camera arrives.
  • For the crew. A guided tour, a large mode for gloved hands, notes on the map, and a printable cuff card.

"Visualize path": the route draws itself over Mount Sharp's boxwork ridges; red hatching is ground too steep for a suit.
